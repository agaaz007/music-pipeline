What these samples establish
----------------------------
Every file in the delivered dataset must meet the same bar as these four:

  1. Separate stems for DRUMS, BASS and SYNTHS, each on its own track,
     with instrument programs/labels that are electronic, not orchestral.
  2. Additional acoustic parts are acceptable when (1) holds.
     sample3 contains piano, violin and harp alongside 1 drum, 1 bass and
     6 synth stems (55% synth notes), and was approved.
  3. Full multi-track arrangement (SMF type 1, one part per track).

Executable form of this rule: musescore_midi/roles.py -> qualifies(path).
All four samples pass it.

What was rejected, for contrast
-------------------------------
The first sample set (not in this repo) was concert-band arrangements of EDM
tracks: 35-36 tracks each of piccolo/flute/oboe/clarinet, 0% synth. The buyer
rejected them: arrangements fine, but GM programs orchestral, so the
instrument labels are unusable for modelling EDM stems.
